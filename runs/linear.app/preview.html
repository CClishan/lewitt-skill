observedrestraint · e-shadows, e-shadows-other, e-home-surface

Depth is a ring, never a shadow ladder

1px rings at low alpha
over
layered box-shadows (0 4px 12px rgba(0,0,0,.3)) on every card

On #08090A a drop shadow is invisible and a strong one muddies the surface; a hairline ring holds the edge without stacking depth.
