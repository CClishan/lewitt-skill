System & taste

observed restraint

Colour is a once-per-page event

Trigger Making a payments brand feel alive without breaking legibility of dense product shots.

Chose a single decorative gradient confined to the hero over gradient text, gradient buttons, and gradient section backgrounds (the common fintech treatment)

Reason Readers need to trust the screenshots more than the scenery; once colour stops, the product becomes the subject.

Guardrail {"forbidCss": [], "forbidClass": ["(^|-)bg-(?:gradient-to-|linear-to-|radial-to-|conic-to-)"], "max": {}, "note": "Needs judgement: allow gradients only inside the hero band and on illustration. Elements at #533AFD are actions, not decoration."}

observed restraint

One indigo, only where you can click

Trigger Deciding how much of a light page the brand colour may occupy.

Chose indigo restricted to actions and links over indigo section backgrounds, indigo icons, and indigo headings

Reason At ~0.2% of the surface, indigo always means 'do something'; a larger share would make the page a brand poster and slow scanning.

Guardrail {"forbidCss": [], "forbidClass": ["(^|-)bg-(indigo|violet|purple)-[4-7]00$", "(^|-)bg-(?:gradient-to-|linear-to-|radial-to-|conic-to-)"], "max": {}, "note": "Allowed on buttons/links only; never on a section or card background."}

observed craft

Light weights carry the headline

Trigger Setting a 48px headline beside a saturated gradient.

Chose variable 300 at every display size over 600-700 headline weights with a 1.33x type ramp

Reason A light face over a heavy gradient leaves the gradient as the loudest thing; bold type would fight the artwork and the screenshots.

observed craft

Blue-black ink instead of grey

Trigger Choosing text colours for a white page with a cold indigo accent.

Chose blue-tinted neutrals over pure greys (#111, #666) or neutral-warm text

Reason Grey text on a white page beside indigo reads as unfinished; shared hue makes the colour look chosen rather than added.

observed system

Motion is tiered by job

Trigger Animating a page with hover states, colour changes and a reveal.

Chose three job-specific durations over one global 200ms ease on everything

Reason Colour has to feel immediate on hover while a layout reveal needs room to read; one duration would make one of them wrong.

Patterns

Text is blue-black, never grey

Headings #061B31 and body #50617A sit on a blue hue axis; muted text is a lighter blue, not a neutral.

Keeps a light page from looking half-finished and ties copy to the indigo CTA. · e-ink, e-ink-body, e-ink-other

One indigo for actions and linked words only

rgb(83,58,253) covers ~0.2% of the surface: filled CTA, 1px outlined CTA, inline links. No indigo surfaces, headers or decoration.

Action colour stays unambiguous while the page stays mostly white. · e-purple, e-canvas-light, e-buttons

One gradient, spent on the hero

A single full-bleed pink/orange/lilac band covers the hero; every section below is flat white or a pale tint.

Delivers a memorable first screen, then removes colour so product detail is readable. · e-hero-band, e-canvas-light, e-sections-tinted

Light weights at every size

300 carries display and body (x302 home, x506 pricing); 400 for UI, 425/500 only inside pricing controls.

Keeps 48px headlines airy so gradient and product shots carry the emotion. · e-weights, e-weights-pricing, e-h1

Controls small, cards large

Buttons and chips 4-6px; cards 16.5px on pricing and 8px elsewhere. No pill buttons in the primary flow.

Distinguishes clickable controls from content containers by silhouette. · e-radii, e-radii-pricing

Shadows tinted to the canvas, not black

Elevation uses rgba(50,50,93,·) plus a second layer, mostly 1-2px tight, with 13-16px blur on floats.

Product screenshots float on white without grey halos. · e-shadow-soft, e-shadow-layered, e-ring

Three motion tiers by job

0.24s for opacity, 0.3s for colour, 0.4s for the grid reveal; reduced-motion and focus-visible are present on the pricing stylesheet.

Colour is instant enough to feel responsive, layout reveals stay calm. · e-motion, e-motion-support

Fixed 128px section rhythm on inner pages

Product pages repeat a 128px gap seven times; the home page uses 160px then a 408px break around the logos.

Inner pages read as one steady column; only the home page gets dramatic spacing. · e-gaps-products, e-gaps-home, e-space

Limitations

  • stripe.com redirects to /en-sg on this network; copy and pricing are the SG locale.
  • The hero gradient is an image (1392x975); its colours are sampled from pixels, not CSS.
  • Rendered font is sohne-var; the loaded face filename was not inspected.
  • Home page CSSOM scan found no :focus-visible/reduced-motion, while the pricing page did — scanning is per-page.
  • contact/sales (page 4) is a short form page and contributes little signal.
