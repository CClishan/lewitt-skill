Patterns

Yellow is an amount, not a mood

Brand yellow is confined to the CTA fill, the hero statistic, the active-tab underline and a few links; it never fills a section or sets body copy.

On a page showing dozens of numbers, yellow can only mean 'this is the figure' or 'this is the action'. · e-brand, e-canvas, e-stroke-strong

Yellow expands with density

Yellow fill is 0.3% of the marketing home page and 5.3% of the trading floor.

Marketing reads dark with a highlight; the trading product reads brand-forward because product mode replaces marketing mode. · e-brand, e-brand-other, e-canvas-futures

Three dark layers, no shadows

Canvas #181A20, card #29313D, well #1E2329, separated by #333B47 hairlines; a single shadow exists in the sample.

Depth survives on OLED and dim screens without blur halos over 12px price text. · e-surface-raised, e-surface-deep, e-stroke-strong, e-shadow

Numbers get the largest type

The hero figure is 72px while headlines are 40px and body 12px — a 6x ratio between statistic and prose.

A reader scanning for a number never hunts; hierarchy is data > headline > body. · e-display-72, e-h2, e-body, e-sizes

One UI family, one earned display face

BinanceNova carries every surface; BinancePlex appears on 10 nodes in 2 of 4 pages.

Keeps product pages uniform while letting campaign pages differ slightly. · e-font-nova, e-font-nova-other, e-font-plex

A two-colour direction axis

Gains use #2EBD85 and losses #F6465D, always as text, never as a filled badge.

Direction stays legible at 12px without competing with the brand yellow. · e-up, e-down

Radii track role

Buttons 10px, cards and tabs 8px, large option tiles 16px, inline chips 4px.

Silhouette alone tells a card from a button from a tile. · e-radii-home, e-radii-markets, e-radii-futures

Hover is fast, panels are slow

Colour and border hovers run at 0.15-0.2s; panel and image transforms at 0.7s.

Interface feedback is immediate while promotional movement stays calm enough to read. · e-motion, e-motion-markets

# Design rules — Binance

<!-- source: https://www.binance.com/en · generated by lewitt from dna.json · do not edit -->

## Hard constraints (from Restraint principles — check these first)

### MUST NOT — Depth is a hairline, not a shadow
_Separate layers with #333B47 1px borders over a #29313D fill; one shadow exists in the whole sample._

- Forbidden class patterns: `shadow-(?!(?:none|sm|inner)\b)[a-z0-9]+`
- Upper bound: `box-shadow` ≤ 0px
- Why: On a dark canvas a shadow is invisible until it is strong enough to smear the 12px digits sitting on it.
- Note: Exception: the single floating overlay panel measured at 14px/6px blur. Everything else is a 1px border.

### MUST NOT — The display face is earned, not standard
_BinanceNova everywhere; BinancePlex only on 10 nodes in 2 of 4 pages._

- Forbidden class patterns: `(^|-)font-(serif|display|mono)$`
- Why: A second family used everywhere splits product identity from marketing; used sparingly it signals that this surface is different.
- Note: A display face is allowed only on campaign blocks, never on tables, prices or controls.

## Design principles

### Colour is assigned by role, not by hierarchy [system]
- Designing a screen that shows prices, price changes, actions and promotions at once. → **a three-role colour system (brand, direction, neutral)** over _a hue per category (per-coin colours, per-section accents, coloured badges)_
- Because: In a list of fifty numbers, a colour that could mean anything stops carrying meaning; three roles keep a glance reliable.
- Evidence: e-brand, e-up, e-down, e-ink

### Depth is a hairline, not a shadow [restraint]
- Layering cards and inputs on a near-black canvas. → **1px hairlines plus a lifted fill** over _a drop shadow on every card and panel_
- Because: On a dark canvas a shadow is invisible until it is strong enough to smear the 12px digits sitting on it.
- Evidence: e-surface-raised, e-stroke-strong, e-shadow

### Data outranks copy [craft]
- Deciding what to enlarge on a landing page that also has to sell a product. → **a 72/40/12 scale biased toward numbers** over _a balanced 48/28/16 marketing scale where copy and data compete_
- Because: The promise of an exchange is a number; making it the largest object lets a screenshot-free page still read as a product.
- Evidence: e-display-72, e-h2, e-body, e-sizes

### The display face is earned, not standard [restraint]
- Choosing type families across marketing and trading surfaces. → **a single UI family with a sparing display face** over _a display/body pairing applied site-wide_
- Because: A second family used everywhere splits product identity from marketing; used sparingly it signals that this surface is different.
- Evidence: e-font-nova, e-font-plex

## System rules

- **Yellow is an amount, not a mood**: Brand yellow is confined to the CTA fill, the hero statistic, the active-tab underline and a few links; it never fills a section or sets body copy.
- **Yellow expands with density**: Yellow fill is 0.3% of the marketing home page and 5.3% of the trading floor.
- **Three dark layers, no shadows**: Canvas #181A20, card #29313D, well #1E2329, separated by #333B47 hairlines; a single shadow exists in the sample.
- **Numbers get the largest type**: The hero figure is 72px while headlines are 40px and body 12px — a 6x ratio between statistic and prose.
- **One UI family, one earned display face**: BinanceNova carries every surface; BinancePlex appears on 10 nodes in 2 of 4 pages.
- **A two-colour direction axis**: Gains use #2EBD85 and losses #F6465D, always as text, never as a filled badge.
- **Radii track role**: Buttons 10px, cards and tabs 8px, large option tiles 16px, inline chips 4px.
- **Hover is fast, panels are slow**: Colour and border hovers run at 0.15-0.2s; panel and image transforms at 0.7s.
