Binance — extracted design system

Three dark layers, one gold accent reserved for action and figures, hairline borders instead of shadows, and a number-first type scale (72px figure against 12px body). Each claim below links to an evidence id in Evidence; anything the gates could not prove is marked unverified rather than asserted.

This reference site is built from ../tokens.css (a deterministic render of dna.json). Nothing here is hand-tuned: if a token changes, every specimen changes with it.

System & taste

observedsystem

Colour is assigned by role, not by hierarchy

Trigger Designing a screen that shows prices, price changes, actions and promotions at once.

Chose a three-role colour system (brand, direction, neutral) over a hue per category (per-coin colours, per-section accents, coloured badges)

Reason In a list of fifty numbers, a colour that could mean anything stops carrying meaning; three roles keep a glance reliable.

observedrestraint

Depth is a hairline, not a shadow

Trigger Layering cards and inputs on a near-black canvas.

Chose 1px hairlines plus a lifted fill over a drop shadow on every card and panel

Reason On a dark canvas a shadow is invisible until it is strong enough to smear the 12px digits sitting on it.

Guardrail {"forbidCss": [], "forbidClass": ["(^|-)shadow-(?!none|sm|inner)[a-z]+-[a-z]+"], "max": {"box-shadow": {"value": "0", "unit": "px"}}, "note": "Exception: the single floating overlay panel measured at 14px/6px blur. Everything else is a 1px border."}

observedcraft

Data outranks copy

Trigger Deciding what to enlarge on a landing page that also has to sell a product.

Chose a 72/40/12 scale biased toward numbers over a balanced 48/28/16 marketing scale where copy and data compete

Reason The promise of an exchange is a number; making it the largest object lets a screenshot-free page still read as a product.

observedrestraint

The display face is earned, not standard

Trigger Choosing type families across marketing and trading surfaces.

Chose a single UI family with a sparing display face over a display/body pairing applied site-wide

Reason A second family used everywhere splits product identity from marketing; used sparingly it signals that this surface is different.

Guardrail {"forbidCss": [], "forbidClass": ["(^|-)font-(serif|display|mono)$"], "max": {}, "note": "A display face is allowed only on campaign blocks, never on tables, prices or controls."}

Patterns

Yellow is an amount, not a mood

Brand yellow is confined to the CTA fill, the hero statistic, the active-tab underline and a few links; it never fills a section or sets body copy.

On a page showing dozens of numbers, yellow can only mean 'this is the figure' or 'this is the action'. · e-brand, e-canvas, e-stroke-strong

Yellow expands with density

Yellow fill is 0.3% of the marketing home page and 5.3% of the trading floor.

Marketing reads dark with a highlight; the trading product reads brand-forward because product mode replaces marketing mode. · e-brand, e-brand-other, e-canvas-futures

Three dark layers, no shadows

Canvas #181A20, card #29313D, well #1E2329, separated by #333B47 hairlines; a single shadow exists in the sample.

Depth survives on OLED and dim screens without blur halos over 12px price text. · e-surface-raised, e-surface-deep, e-stroke-strong, e-shadow

Numbers get the largest type

The hero figure is 72px while headlines are 40px and body 12px — a 6x ratio between statistic and prose.

A reader scanning for a number never hunts; hierarchy is data > headline > body. · e-display-72, e-h2, e-body, e-sizes

One UI family, one earned display face

BinanceNova carries every surface; BinancePlex appears on 10 nodes in 2 of 4 pages.

Keeps product pages uniform while letting campaign pages differ slightly. · e-font-nova, e-font-nova-other, e-font-plex

A two-colour direction axis

Gains use #2EBD85 and losses #F6465D, always as text, never as a filled badge.

Direction stays legible at 12px without competing with the brand yellow. · e-up, e-down

Radii track role

Buttons 10px, cards and tabs 8px, large option tiles 16px, inline chips 4px.

Silhouette alone tells a card from a button from a tile. · e-radii-home, e-radii-markets, e-radii-futures

Hover is fast, panels are slow

Colour and border hovers run at 0.15-0.2s; panel and image transforms at 0.7s.

Interface feedback is immediate while promotional movement stays calm enough to read. · e-motion, e-motion-markets

Limitations

  • binance.com redirects to www.binance.com/en; output folder keeps the requested binance.com name.
  • Menu dropdowns and modals were not opened (reversible-state risk); their states are unknown.
  • The homepage h1 is a 16px utility node, so display sizes were read from the hero figure and h2, not semantic tags.
  • prefers-reduced-motion was found on 2 of 4 pages; the CSSOM scan disagrees page to page.
  • BinancePlex appears on only 2 of 4 pages, so its role is inferred.
