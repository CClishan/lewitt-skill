# Design rules — Steno.ai

<!-- source: https://www.steno.ai/ · generated by lewitt from dna.json · do not edit -->

## Hard constraints (from Restraint principles — check these first)

### MUST NOT — The button slip is the whole interaction
_On hover the button's inner fill resets its transform (translate 0 / rotate 0) over 525ms with cubic-bezier(0.625, 0.05, 0, 1); the label does not move._

- Forbidden class patterns: `hover:shadow-(?!(?:none|sm|inner)\b)[a-z0-9]+`
- Upper bound: `box-shadow` ≤ 0px
- Why: A page already carrying an image is already loud (the restrained motion is a slip, not a raise); the icon fill turning a shade lighter tells the pointer 'active' without a second focal point.
- Note: The control's only motion is the measured 525ms transform slip; a hover shadow is a violation.

## Design principles

### The button slip is the whole interaction [restraint]
- Deciding how a filled CTA reacts to the pointer on a page whose hero is a moving image. → **one accent-brown slip inside the 8px frame** over _lift/shadow and scale on the whole button, or a second accent colour on the fill_
- Because: A page already carrying an image is already loud (the restrained motion is a slip, not a raise); the icon fill turning a shade lighter tells the pointer 'active' without a second focal point.
- Evidence: e-motion-btn, e-motion-hover-rule, e-action-bg

### Photo is the only colour cut [craft]
- Deciding where the accent may appear on a hero that carries a full-size photograph. → **neon spot inside an icon + brand card on ink** over _neon-filled CTA, or #2D62FF primary buttons on every section_
- Because: Amber grass and teal dusk raise the luminance of every near region of the hero; a chromatic fill at button scale would flatten the lightness contrast the serif headline relies on.
- Evidence: e-accent-lime, e-action-bg, e-ink-soft

### Grain and grain only are allowed to move [craft]
- Deciding what animates when the page opens and when a pointer reaches a control. → **one transform curve with 0.3s opacity support** over _spring/bounce easings and staggered keyframes per element_
- Because: The hero's frame and copy float in one timeline, so extra easing on any child just adds a second rhythm; one curve keeps copy and card reading as one.
- Evidence: e-motion-btn, e-motion-soft

### Grain without motion drift [system]
- Running under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce (zero blocks on any of the four pages). → **the same timeline with 0.3s opacity support** over _progressing drift staying in place_
- Because: The page reads as a document; a drifting parallax under a document is a visual defect, not an effect to slow down, so a build that mirrors this site must add the branch the site is missing.
- Evidence: e-motion-reduced-scan, e-motion-btn, e-motion-soft

## System rules

- **Cinematic sheet, streaming landscape**: The hero is one full-width landscape frame (653×512 video still, inset 1px) with a piece of copy pinned to its left; product cards become hashed collage slices (rotate(−6°/+4°)) rather than floating UI.
- **Oatmeal-only palette**: Both light and dark are the same hue: #F0EDDA / #282724, with #FFFDF1, #CFCCB1, #DAD7C1 as bands and #BAA57B as the one product surface; #CAFF75 is the sole chromatic accent (spot).
- **Paper-serif hero, sans everywhere else**: DISPLAY text is PP Writer 400 (no weight heavier than 400); interface and labels are PP Mori 400, with 600 only for the label inside filled CTA buttons.
- **8px softness everywhere except the quote pill**: Controls, cards and the hero frame share radius 8px; pills and option cards use 1600px to their role; shadow is an edge, not a depth cue.
- **Neon is a spot, never a surface**: #CAFF75 appears only inside the hero eyebrow glyph (a disk behind a triangular play mark); no background, border or text of this hue is allowed.
